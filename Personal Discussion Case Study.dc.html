One structured interview for every applicant.

Before a case reaches CAM, the credit manager speaks with the borrower and each co-borrower. The PD tab turns that conversation into a guided set of questions, scores and flags — the same shape for every applicant, every time.

Executive summary
A free-form conversation became a scored, comparable record.

01Problem & opportunity

Good conversations, hard to compare.

PD is where a credit manager tests what the application claims — why this course, how the family will repay, what the bureau report doesn’t show. Without a shared structure, every manager asked their own questions and wrote it up their own way. Reviewers at CAM had to read paragraphs to find the risk, and two cases were hard to put side by side.

Opportunity

Give every PD the same backbone — same sections, same questions, same scale — while leaving room for the manager’s judgement in their own words.

02Approach

A checklist the manager can still think in.

Four principles kept the structure from turning the interview into data entry.

Principle 01 Same backbone for everyone Nine sections in a fixed order, for the borrower and every co-borrower alike.
Principle 02 Start from what we know Institute, course, exam and score arrive pre-filled. The manager confirms and adds context instead of re-typing.
Principle 03 Score next to the evidence The rating closes each section, right after its answers — never on a separate summary screen.
Principle 04 Never lose a conversation Every change saves in the background, with Saving and Saved shown in the header.
Key design decisions
01One tab per applicant, each with its own statusPending and submitted are visible without opening anyone — the manager always knows who is left.
02Accordions plus a jump barA long interview, but any section is one click away, and each collapsed row shows how many questions are answered.
03Colour the rating on the collapsed rowGreen, amber and red make weak areas scannable before CAM — without reopening a single section.
04Validate on submit, inlineMissing answers are flagged where they sit, and collapsed sections that hold gaps are tinted so nothing hides.

04Outcomes

Every PD now reaches CAM in the same shape.

05Learnings

Structure works when it gets out of the way.

01A form can still be a conversationPre-filled answers and a comment per question let the manager keep talking instead of typing.
02Scores need their reasons beside themA number on its own invites second-guessing. Next to its answers, a reviewer can see why it was given.
03Autosave is a trust featurePDs happen on calls that drop. Showing Saving and Saved removes the worry of losing an interview halfway.

Overview

We redesigned education lending as one connected journey — from the first eligibility check to the last EMI.

The studies cover the app redesign, DigiLocker verification, Account Aggregator consent, OCR validation, post-disbursement servicing and multi-loan applications. Each solved a distinct moment; together they created continuity from first intent to long-term servicing.

01
Reduce cognitive load

One decision per screen, defaults that match the profile, and progress that is always visible.

02
Make trust visible

Show what is shared, why, and for how long — and name the limits as clearly as the ask.

03
Design for the full lifecycle

Patterns that carry from application to repayment, so every stage feels like the same product.

01Problem & strategic context

82.6% of students were walking away before submitting.

Kuhoo’s eligibility form was the single gateway to a ₹18.4L median loan. Three months of Mixpanel data, 140 PostHog session recordings, a heuristic review and moderated testing told the same story: the product was losing students faster than marketing could acquire them.

Business case

April–August carries 62% of annual volume. Doubling completion before peak season wasn’t a design aspiration — it was revenue recovery. The opportunity was already funded; we were leaving it on the table.

02Strategy & constraints

Fix flow before surface. Never lose student work.

Hard constraints shaped every decision: mandated regulatory fields, an incomplete vendor catalogue, no backend rewrite, the existing brand, and four weeks to a dev-ready handoff.

LOS · 02 · Part payments & servicing Loan origination system · Post disbursement

Part payments, simulated before they’re booked.

Once a loan is disbursed, customers still call — to prepay part of it, to check their schedule, to ask for a certificate. The Post Disbursement tab gives the operations team one place for all three, and every part payment is simulated and reviewed before it changes the loan.

Executive summary
A prepayment desk inside the loan file.

Enter an amount and date, compare two revised schedules, record how the money arrived, then confirm. Each finished step locks behind an Edit button, and every booking lands in a history the whole team can read.

01Context

Servicing, without leaving the file.

A part payment changes the loan: either the tenure gets shorter or the EMI gets smaller, and the customer usually wants to know which before they pay. The team needed to show both outcomes, record the payment against the right reference and keep a trail — on the same application page they already use for everything else.

Where it lives

A last tab in the application, after Disbursement — with three sub-tabs for the jobs that come after the money moves.

03Repayment schedule

A schedule that grows sideways.

Education loans move through pre-EMI and EMI periods, each with its own rate, repayment type and amount. Periods are columns and attributes are rows, so a loan with six periods reads the same way as one with two.

04Other actions

Three documents, one click each.

The documents customers ask for most sit as three cards: an interest certificate by financial year, a statement of account, and the no-objection certificate needed to release papers.

05Key design decisions

Show the result before the commitment.

01Simulate before anything is savedBoth revised schedules are calculated from the amount and date alone, so the customer can decide on the call.
02Recommend, don’t decideReduce tenure carries a Recommended tag, but both options sit side by side with interest saved on each.
03Lock each step, keep an EditFinished cards grey out with a single Edit button, so the review always matches what was entered.
04Ask only for what the method needsA payment link takes a reference ID; a bank transfer asks for the bank and UTR number — nothing else.
06Outcomes

Part payments booked on the first call.

With both outcomes simulated in the tab and every step locked before review, the team stopped calculating schedules offline and correcting bookings afterwards. First 12 weeks after rollout vs the 12 weeks before; figures are directional.
